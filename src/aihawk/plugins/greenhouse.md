The page shows the job and, below it, the form. There is no need to click "Apply": the fields are already on the page. Do not use "Autofill my application", Dropbox or Google Drive.

`#first_name`, `#last_name`, `#email` and `#phone` are the basics. Upload the CV to `#resume`. `#cover_letter` is a file too: leave it empty unless it is required.

Questions a company adds have ids such as `#question_39108683002`: find each by its label. Many fields look like text but are lists (`role="combobox"`, showing "Select..."), such as `#country` and `#candidate-location`. Type the value, then click the suggestion that appears, and read the page to check it was taken: text left unselected is not an answer.

Older pages at boards.greenhouse.io have the same fields in a simpler layout. The submit control is the button "Submit application".
